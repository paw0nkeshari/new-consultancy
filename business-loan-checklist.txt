BUSINESS LOAN DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Commonly requested items (lender-specific):
- KYC and address proof of applicant/promoters
- PAN and entity constitution documents
- Bank statements
- GST returns and ITRs, where applicable
- Financial statements and provisional results
- Existing loan details and repayment track
- CMA data / projected cash flows, if requested
- Project report, quotations and use-of-funds details
- Collateral/title documents, if applicable

This is indicative only. A lender decides final eligibility and documentation.
