MONTHLY BOOKKEEPING CHECKLIST

- Record sales and purchase invoices
- Record expenses and employee reimbursements
- Reconcile bank and cash balances
- Review receivables and payables ageing
- Reconcile GST ledgers and returns data
- Review inventory and fixed assets
- Check loan balances and interest entries
- Review payroll and statutory deductions
- Close the month and archive supporting documents

Adapt this list to your accounting policy and applicable requirements.
