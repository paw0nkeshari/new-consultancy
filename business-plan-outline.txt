BUSINESS PLAN OUTLINE

1. Executive summary
2. Promoter / management profile
3. Product or service description
4. Target customer and market opportunity
5. Competitor and positioning analysis
6. Sales and marketing plan
7. Operations and staffing plan
8. Project cost and funding requirement
9. Revenue, cost and cash-flow projections
10. Risks, assumptions and mitigation
11. Implementation milestones
