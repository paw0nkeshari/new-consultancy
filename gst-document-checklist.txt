GST DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Typical information to discuss with your tax professional:
- PAN of proprietor/entity
- Proof of constitution (as applicable)
- Identity and address proof of promoters/authorised signatory
- Principal place of business proof
- Bank account proof, where required
- Business activity and HSN/SAC details
- Sales, purchase and expense records for return preparation
- Previous GST returns and notices, if any

This is a general checklist. Requirements vary by case and current GST rules. Verify with an authorised professional.
